At 300 K , $x$ moles of $\mathrm{CaCl_2}$ ($i$ = 2.5 ; molar mass = 111 g mol$^{-1}$) is dissolved in 2.5 L of water.

The osmotic pressure of resultant solution is 0.75 atm. What is $\Delta \mathrm{T_b}$ of solution ?

(density of water = 1g mL$^{-1}$ ; $\mathrm{K_b}$ = 0.52 K kg mol$^{-1}$ ; R = 0.08 L atm mol$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$)

  1. (1)

    0.016 K

  2. (2)

    0.032 K

  3. (3)

    0.048 K

  4. (4)

    0.064 K

Show answer

Option (1) is correct.
