AP EAPCET 2020 (22nd Sep 2020 Shift1) Mathematics: Circles

Multiple choice, one correct option AP EAPCET paper Circles

If the circles $x^{2} + y^{2} - 2x - 2y - 7 = 0$ and $x^{2} + y^{2} + 4x + 2y + k = 0$ cut orthogonally, then the length of their common chord is ______ units

  1. (1)

    2

  2. (2)

    5

  3. (3)

    $\dfrac{6}{\sqrt{13}}$

  4. (4)

    $\dfrac{12}{\sqrt{13}}$

Show answer

Option (4) is correct.
