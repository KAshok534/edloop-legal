In which of the processes, the entropy will decrease

  1. (1)

    $\mathrm{H_2\,(g) \rightarrow 2H(g)}$

  2. (2)

    Liquid water crystallizes to ice

  3. (3)

    Temperature of ice is raised from 10 K to 115 K

  4. (4)

    $\mathrm{2NaHCO_3\,(s) \rightarrow Na_2CO_3\,(s) + CO_2\,(g) + H_2O\,(s)}$

Show answer

Option (2) is correct.
