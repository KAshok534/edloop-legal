How often it comes up

Definite Integration is one of the chapters JEE Main Mathematics returns to in almost every paper. In the papers we hold from 2019 to 2026 it gave 248 questions in 162 papers, about 1.5 questions per paper, and it appeared in 153 of those papers. By share of Mathematics questions (5.4%) it ranks 5th of 29 Mathematics chapters in that period.

It has grown since the AIEEE years. From 2002 to 2018 it was 4.8% of Mathematics; in the last three exam years (2024 to 2026) it is 5.6%, with 86 questions in 58 papers. The busiest year was 2021, with 49 questions across the 26 papers we hold. Since 2019 there has been no year without it.

It is a Class 12 chapter, part of NTA's Unit 8: Integral Calculus, and it sits on top of Indefinite Integration: if your antiderivatives are slow, these questions will be slow too.

Most-asked topics

Almost all the questions fall under three topics. From 2019 to 2026:

  • Evaluation & Properties: 198 questions. Most of the chapter: evaluating an integral directly or, more often, using a property to avoid the long route.
  • Special Definite Integrals: 30 questions. Integral equations, functions defined by an integral, and integrals that hide a known result.
  • Limit as a Sum & Estimation: 20 questions. Turning a limit of a sum into an integral, or bounding an integral.

All three topics were asked again in 2026.

What the questions look like

The typical question gives an integral that looks heavy and rewards the student who spots the trick: an odd function over a symmetric interval, the property that replaces x with a + b − x, a modulus that splits the interval, or the greatest integer or fractional part function that turns the integral into a sum of simple pieces.

A second kind defines a function through an integral and asks for its value somewhere. These need you to differentiate both sides (the fundamental theorem of calculus) and solve the small equation that results.

The third kind starts as a limit of a sum, and the work is in recognising the Riemann sum and writing the right integral.

A large share is numerical-answer: 73 of the 248 questions since 2019 had no options, so the final value has to be exact.

Questions to try first

From NTA's own 2026 papers, one for each kind of question above:

  • A symmetric-interval integral with a modulus (JEE Main 2026, 21 Jan Shift 1)
  • An integral with the greatest integer function (JEE Main 2026, 22 Jan Shift 1)
  • A function defined by an integral: find f(2) − f(3) (JEE Main 2026, 22 Jan Shift 1)
  • A limit of a sum written as an integral (JEE Main 2026, 21 Jan Shift 2, numerical answer)
  • An integral equation for f with an exponential term (JEE Main 2026, 24 Jan Shift 2, numerical answer)

How to practise it

Make a short list of the standard properties and test yourself on when each one applies; most marks in this chapter come from choosing the right property in the first minute. Then practise integrals with the modulus, greatest integer and fractional part functions until splitting the interval is automatic.

After that, do the integral-equation questions together with Differential Equations practice, since the method overlaps. Keep limit-as-a-sum questions for a separate short set: there are fewer of them, but they are quick marks once the pattern is familiar.

The full list below goes year by year, so you can also work through one recent year at a time as a timed set.

Frequently asked questions

Is Definite Integration important for JEE Main 2027?

Yes. It gave 248 questions in the 162 papers we hold from 2019 to 2026, about 1.5 per paper, ranks 5th of 29 Mathematics chapters by share of questions, and appeared every year in that period.

Which part of Definite Integration is asked the most?

Evaluation and properties of definite integrals, with 198 of the 248 questions from 2019 to 2026. Special definite integrals (30) and limit as a sum (20) make up the rest.

Is Definite Integration different from Indefinite Integration in JEE Main?

They are separate chapters, but Definite Integration depends on fast antiderivatives. Practise Indefinite Integration first, then the properties that make definite integrals short.
