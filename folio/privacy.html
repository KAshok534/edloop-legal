Folio Privacy Policy

For the Folio app (Android, package app.health.personal). Last updated 11 November 2026.

On this page

In short. Folio is a personal health journal. You sign in with Google, and the things you log (weight, food, water, sleep, mood, symptoms, medicines, exercise, photos and reports you add) are kept in your own private account so they follow you to a new phone. We do not show ads, we do not sell or share your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app. Folio is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Who we are

Folio is published by EDLOOP TECHNOLOGIES, a business registered in India (MSME, Udyam), which is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Folio is separate from the EdLoop JEE and EdLoop EAPCET apps; it has its own policy, and the EdLoop apps' policy does not apply to Folio.

Contact for privacy questions and requests about your data: ashokkumar.kunchala@edloop.in (Ashok Kumar Kunchala, Founder & CTO).

We do not collect your location, contacts, files you did not choose to add, or your advertising identifier. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Folio can read steps, sleep, heart rate, exercise sessions and weight. Folio never writes anything back to Health Connect. You decide which of those types to allow, and you can change or withdraw that at any time in Health Connect or on the Folio Settings screen.

Data from Health Connect is used only to show you your own readings and trends inside Folio. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else. Readings that are in your journal may be included in the AI summaries described below when you use an AI feature.

AI features

Folio has AI features: an assistant you can chat with, a short daily note about your own entries, reading a meal from its text or photo, and summarising a report you add. When you use one, the text, photo or the small summary of your entries needed for that request is sent from the app to our server and on to an AI provider to produce the answer. The providers we use are Anthropic (Claude) first, with Groq and Google (Gemini) as backups if the first is unavailable.

  • The AI keys are kept on our server, never inside the app.
  • We do not send your email address to the AI providers. Your first name and the profile details needed to answer (for example age, height and goal) may be included so the reply can address you and make sense.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not keep the AI requests or answers on our servers. The answers are shown to you and, where you save an entry, kept in your journal.
  • The AI providers process the request under their own terms. AI answers can be wrong, so please do not treat them as medical advice.

How it is stored

  • Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and storage provider, which processes it on our behalf.
  • Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and the server.
  • Each account can read and change only its own data. Photos and reports are stored in a private folder for your account.
  • A copy is kept on your phone so the app works without a connection; it is uploaded when the connection returns.

How it is used and shared

  • No sale, no sharing, no ads. Your data is used only to run Folio for you.
  • It is shared only with the service providers named on this page (Supabase to store it; the AI providers only for the AI requests you make), and only as far as needed to provide those services.
  • We do not use your health data to show you or anyone else advertising.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and everything in it: in Folio, open Index → Settings and account → Delete account. This removes your profile, all entries, photos and reports from our servers and from the phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Can't open the app? Write to ashokkumar.kunchala@edloop.in from the email address you signed in with and we will delete your account and data.
  • Export: you can export your journal from the Archive screen.
  • Health Connect and reminders: switch either off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Google access: you can see or remove the access Folio has to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Folio only asks for your name, email address and profile picture.

Children

Folio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If advertising or paid features are ever added, this policy will be updated before they are released.
