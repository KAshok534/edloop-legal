Folio — a personal health journal

A quiet Android app for keeping your own health records in one place, kept by hand.

What it is. Folio helps you log your weight, meals, water, sleep, mood, symptoms, medicines and exercise, and see how they change over time. You sign in with Google and your journal follows you to a new phone.

What Folio does

  • Track by hand: weight, food and water, sleep, mood, symptoms, medicines and exercise, with charts and plain-language notes.
  • Health Connect: optionally read steps, sleep, heart rate and exercise from your phone or watch. Folio only reads; it never writes back.
  • Photos and reports: keep progress photos and medical reports in your private account.
  • AI help: an assistant and daily note about your own entries, and reading a meal from a photo. AI answers can be wrong and are not medical advice.
  • Reminders: optional gentle nudges to weigh in, drink water and check in.

Availability

Folio is currently in private testing on Android and is not yet on Google Play.

Your data

We do not show ads and we do not sell or share your data. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app. Read the Folio Privacy Policy for the details.
