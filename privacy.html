In short. You sign in with Google. We keep your name, email address and practice history on our servers so your progress follows you to a new phone. We do not show ads, we do not sell or share your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Who we are

EdLoop is a brand of EDLOOP TECHNOLOGIES, a business registered in India (MSME, Udyam). EDLOOP TECHNOLOGIES publishes the EdLoop JEE app on Google Play and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy questions and requests about your data: ashokkumar.kunchala@edloop.in (Ashok Kumar Kunchala, Founder & CTO). For help with the app: support@edloop.in.

What we collect

Data Examples Why
Account details from Google Your name, email address and a Google account identifier; your EdLoop ID, a random code we give your account (shown to you in Settings; it says nothing about you) To create your account and sign you in
Your profile (optional) The name you choose to be called in the app; an avatar you pick from the app's own pictures (never a photo); a mobile number, if you add one To greet you by your name, and to show it to the others in a live test room you join; to contact you about your account or EdLoop. The mobile number is never shown in the app, never shared and never used for advertising, and you can change or remove it in Settings
Practice activity Questions you answered and how, time taken, timed tests and scores, bookmarks, notes To show your progress and restore it on another phone
Live test rooms (only if you make or join one) The rooms you make or join, the answers you give during the race, your score and rank To run the race and show everyone in the room the results. Finished rooms are deleted after 30 days
Friends (only if you add one) Who you are friends with, friend requests you send or get, people you block or report, and invites to a live test room So you and your friends can invite each other to live test rooms. Friends are added only by their exact EdLoop ID or their own link (never by searching names) and only when both agree. Unanswered requests are deleted after 30 days; reports are read by EdLoop
Notifications (only if you allow them) A notification token from Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) for your phone To tell you when a friend sends a request, accepts yours, invites you to a room or takes your challenge. It is deleted when you sign out or delete your account; turn notifications off any time in your phone's settings
Challenges, invites and your week (only if you use them) Challenges you make or take (your answers, score and time); who brought you to EdLoop, if you installed it from someone's link; how many questions you answered this week To rank everyone who takes a challenge; to unlock avatars for the person who invited you once you have practised; to show you and your friends this week's counts. Challenges are deleted 30 days after they close
Settings Selected exam, target year, daily goal, optional streak, your own exam date So the app looks the same after you sign in again
Daily use counts How many new questions, answer keys and solutions your account opened each day To apply fair-use daily limits and stop automated copying of the question bank
Error reports you send The question, the kind of error, and any text you type To correct the question bank

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files or advertising identifier, and we do not read your phone number from your phone (we only keep one if you type it in). We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

How it is stored

  • Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it on our behalf in its data centre in Mumbai, India.
  • Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and the server.
  • Each account can read and change only its own data.
  • A copy is kept on your phone so the app stays fast and an answer is never lost when the connection drops; it is uploaded when the connection returns. Questions you have not opened for a while are removed from the phone and downloaded again when needed.

How it is used and shared

  • Used only to run the app for you and to improve the accuracy of the question bank.
  • We may compute combined statistics across all students, such as how many answered a question correctly. These never identify a student.
  • Question text delivered to your account carries an invisible mark linked to the account, so that copies of the question bank published elsewhere can be traced.
  • In a live test room, the others in that room see the name you go by in EdLoop and your avatar, how many questions you have answered during the race, and your score and rank when it ends. They never see your email address or mobile number. Names with abusive words are not shown. Reactions you send are shown to the room and are not stored.
  • Your friends on EdLoop see your EdLoop ID, your first name and your avatar, and the room you invite them to. Nobody can find you by your name. You can remove, block or report anyone at any time. Your friends also see how many questions you answered this week.
  • Anyone who takes a challenge you made (or that you took) sees the first name, avatar, score and time of everyone who finished it, once they have finished it too.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties, other than the providers that run the service for us (Google for sign-in, Supabase for storage).
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and all of the data above from our servers and from your phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Clear your progress only: Settings → Clear progress.
  • Sign out: Settings → Sign out. Your data stays in your account.
  • If you cannot use the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page or write to the address above from the email address of your account, and we will delete it within 7 days.

YouTube

This section covers EdLoop’s own channel on YouTube (@edloop_in) and the tools our team uses to run it. It is separate from the app: the EdLoop JEE app does not use YouTube API Services and takes nothing from YouTube.

EdLoop uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) only for our own channel, and only to: publish our own videos to our own channel; read the comments on our own videos and reply to them; and read our own channel’s statistics. The connection is made by EdLoop with the EdLoop channel’s own Google account, through automated jobs that we run. No viewer, student or app user is asked to sign in to or authorise anything for it, and we never access anyone else’s YouTube account, videos, subscriptions or private data. The access we ask Google for is limited to the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.

By interacting with our YouTube content (watching, liking, commenting) you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

What we access What we do with it What we keep, and for how long
Comments on our videos: the public comment text, the commenter’s public display name and public channel ID, and the time Our team reads them so we can answer viewers. An AI assistant sorts them and drafts suggested replies for our founder to approve. We post a reply by ourselves only to a clearly positive comment such as “Great app, thanks!”, and that reply is always one of a few fixed thank-you lines. Every other reply is posted only after a person has approved it The comment text is held only briefly, for replying, in a temporary file that is deleted within 3 days. We keep the comment ID and whether we have handled it, so that we never reply twice. For each reply we send we keep a private log entry (time, comment ID, our reply and, for automatic thank-yous, the commenter’s public display name)
Our own channel’s statistics: subscriber, video and view totals A weekly internal report that helps us see which videos students find useful The totals, in the weekly report. They describe our channel, not any person
Our own videos: the video file, title and description we upload We publish short educational videos (for example, JEE Main past-question tips and app announcements) to our channel, on a schedule our founder approves each week The video link and ID, in our posting record
  • No sale, no sharing, no ads. We do not sell or rent YouTube data, we do not use it for advertising or to build profiles of people, and we do not use it to train or fine-tune any AI model. We share it with no one except the providers that run the service for us: GitHub, which hosts our private repository and automated jobs, and Anthropic, whose AI assistant sorts the comments and drafts replies. Access is limited to the EdLoop team.
  • Care with young viewers. Many of our viewers are students under 18. We never ask anyone for personal details, we never send private messages, and a comment that gives or asks for personal details (such as age, school, city or phone number) is never answered automatically.
  • Ask us to delete it. To have a comment, a reply or anything else we hold about you from YouTube deleted, write to support@edloop.in and tell us the video and the comment. We delete it within 7 days. The comment itself stays on YouTube until you delete it there.
  • Revoke access. If you are a Google user and want to see or remove the access that any application has to your Google account, go to https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Viewers have granted our YouTube connection no access, so there is nothing for them to revoke; the EdLoop team can revoke it at any time on the same page.

Children

EdLoop is meant for students preparing for engineering entrance examinations, typically 16 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. Students under 18 should use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

For parents and guardians

Most people who use EdLoop are students preparing for JEE Main, and many of them are under 18. In plain words, this is what EdLoop does with your child’s data.

  • What we keep: the name and email address from their Google sign-in, the name they choose to be called and an avatar from the app’s own pictures (never a photo), their practice and test history, and a mobile number only if they type one in. We do not collect their location, contacts, photos or files.
  • No ads, no tracking: EdLoop shows no advertising and has no analytics or advertising software. We do not build a profile of your child for anyone, and we do not sell or share their data.
  • Who can see them: no one, unless they make or join a live test room. In a room, the others see only the name they go by and their avatar, their progress in that test, and their score and rank. Never their email address or mobile number. Names with abusive words are not shown, and finished rooms are deleted after 30 days. Friends are added only with their exact EdLoop ID or their own link and only when both agree; a friend sees just a first name and an avatar, and anyone can be blocked or reported.
  • No messages between students: students cannot send each other messages in EdLoop. In a room they can only send a fixed set of quick reactions, which are not stored.
  • Deleting the account: your child can delete the account and all its data in the app (Settings → Delete account). You can also ask us to delete it: write to ashokkumar.kunchala@edloop.in with the email address of the account. We confirm the request with that address and delete the account within 7 days.
  • Questions: write to the same address. We are happy to explain anything on this page.
