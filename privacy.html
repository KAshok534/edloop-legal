EdLoop

Privacy policy

Applies to the Android app EdLoop (EdLoop JEE). Last updated 8 October 2026.

Summary

You sign in with Google. We keep your name, email address and your practice history on our servers so that your progress follows you to a new phone. We do not show ads, we do not sell or share your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Who we are

EdLoop is operated by Ashok Kumar Kunchala, India. Contact: ashokkunchala.work@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataExamplesWhy
Account details from GoogleYour name, email address and a Google account identifierTo create your account and sign you in
Practice activityQuestions you answered and how, time taken, test sessions and scores, bookmarks, notesTo show your progress and restore it on another device
SettingsSelected exam, target year, daily goal, optional streak setting, your own exam dateSo the app looks the same after you sign in again
Error reports you sendThe question, the kind of error, and any text you typeTo correct the question bank

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, phone number or device advertising identifier. We do not use analytics or advertising software.

How it is stored

  • Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it on our behalf in its data centre in Mumbai, India.
  • Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and the server.
  • Each account can read and change only its own data.
  • A copy is kept on your phone so the app stays fast and so an answer is never lost when the connection drops. It is uploaded as soon as the connection returns.

How it is used and shared

  • Used only to run the app for you and to improve the accuracy of the question bank.
  • We may compute combined statistics across all students, such as how many answered a question correctly. These never identify a student.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties, other than the providers that run the service for us (Google for sign-in, Supabase for storage).
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and all of the data above from our servers and from your phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Clear your progress only: Settings → Clear progress.
  • Sign out: Settings → Sign out. Your data stays in your account.
  • If you cannot use the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page, or write to the address above from the email address of your account, and we will delete it within 7 days.

Children

The app is meant for students preparing for engineering entrance examinations, typically 15 and older. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 18, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If paid features or advertising are ever added, this policy will be updated before they are released.
