Kinematics · Uniform acceleration

Starting from rest, a particle accelerates uniformly. It covers 7 m during the fourth second. How far does it travel during the third second?

Choose your answer

Distance in the nth second

For a start from rest, sₙ = a(n − ½). So 7 = a × 3.5, giving a = 2 m/s². During the third second: 2 × 2.5 = 5 m.
