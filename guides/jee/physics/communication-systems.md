## How often it comes up

NTA removed Communication Systems from the JEE Main syllabus in 2024. Across all papers we hold from 2011 to 2023, it contributed **104 questions across 200 papers**, appearing in 103 of those papers. From 2019 to 2026, it gave 82 questions in 162 papers, about 0.5 questions per paper.

Before its removal, the chapter was asked regularly. In the AIEEE and early JEE Main years (2002 to 2018) it gave 22 questions in 38 papers, making up 1.6% of Physics questions. In our set it was last asked in 2023.

It was historically taught as a Class 12 chapter under Communication Systems (removed 2024).

Questions in Communication Systems test both fundamental concepts and direct calculations. Students encounter problems that combine core definitions with multi-step algebraic or physical setups.

In **Modulation**, questions focus on standard exam setups. Success on these questions depends on understanding the underlying rules rather than relying on rote formulas.

In **Communication Basics**, questions focus on standard exam setups. Success on these questions depends on understanding the underlying rules rather than relying on rote formulas.
