## How to practise it

Begin your preparation by thoroughly reviewing the core concepts and definitions from standard textbooks. Build a structured summary sheet containing essential formulas, definitions, and sign conventions for Electrochemistry.

Next, work through past JEE Main questions topic by topic. Start with Electrochemical Cells, which provides the foundation for the rest of the chapter. Solve problems without consulting hints or answer keys on your first attempt.
