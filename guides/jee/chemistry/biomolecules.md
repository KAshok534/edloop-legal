## How to practise it

Begin your preparation by thoroughly reviewing the core concepts and definitions from standard textbooks. Build a structured summary sheet containing essential formulas, definitions, and sign conventions for Biomolecules.

Next, work through past JEE Main questions topic by topic. Start with Carbohydrates, which provides the foundation for the rest of the chapter. Solve problems without consulting hints or answer keys on your first attempt.

Focus on precision during numerical practice. Because numerical-answer questions require exact values, pay close attention to rounding rules, powers of ten, and unit conversions throughout your solving process.

Finally, take timed practice sets combining questions from different years. This builds exam-day stamina and helps you recognize question patterns quickly under time constraints.
