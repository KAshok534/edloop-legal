Following the curriculum reduction in 2024, the chapter saw 0 questions across 58 papers in the 2024 to 2026 period. Consequently, its overall trajectory has trended down.

## Most-asked topics

Historic papers distributed questions across three specific topics:

- Colloids: Generated 72 total questions (55 during 2019 to 2026), last evaluated in 2023. Areas covered included classification of sols, lyophilic versus lyophobic behaviors, Hardy-Schulze rule for coagulation, electrophoresis, and micelle formation.
- Adsorption: Accounted for 46 total questions (33 during 2019 to 2026), last tested in 2023. Core concepts focused on distinguishing physisorption from chemisorption, thermodynamic parameters (enthalpy and entropy changes), and the Freundlich adsorption isotherm.
- Catalysis: Yielded 12 total questions (10 during 2019 to 2026), last asked in 2023. Questions examined homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis, enzyme kinetics curves, and intermediate compound theories.

## What the questions look like

Past problems were predominantly conceptual and descriptive, demanding accurate recall of terminology. Typical items asked students to contrast physisorption against chemisorption based on reversibility, temperature influence, or enthalpy values. In colloidal systems, questions regularly tested the coagulating power of various electrolytes on positively or negatively charged sols.

Mathematical questions were restricted almost entirely to the Freundlich isotherm equation, evaluating logarithmic plots of x/m versus pressure to deduce constants.
