## How often it comes up

NTA removed s-Block Elements from the JEE Main syllabus in 2024. Across all papers we hold from 2002 to 2023, it contributed **130 questions across 200 papers**, appearing in 111 of those papers. From 2019 to 2026, it gave 104 questions in 162 papers, about 0.6 questions per paper.

Before its removal, the chapter was asked regularly. In the AIEEE and early JEE Main years (2002 to 2018) it gave 26 questions in 38 papers, making up 1.9% of Chemistry questions. In our set it was last asked in 2023.

It was historically taught as a Class 11 chapter under s-Block Elements (removed 2024).
