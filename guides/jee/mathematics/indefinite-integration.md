## How to practise it

Begin your preparation by thoroughly reviewing the core concepts and definitions from standard textbooks. Build a structured summary sheet containing essential formulas, definitions, and sign conventions for Indefinite Integration.

Next, work through past JEE Main questions topic by topic. Start with Standard Integrals & Substitution, which provides the foundation for the rest of the chapter. Solve problems without consulting hints or answer keys on your first attempt.
