## How often it comes up

Up until the end of the 2023 examination season, mathematical reasoning appeared in most testing sessions, delivering multiple-choice questions on symbolic logic. However, following the curriculum overhaul by the NTA for the 2024 exam, the chapter was completely removed.

In 2024, 2025, and 2026, exactly zero questions appeared across all 58 examination sessions. As a result, candidates preparing for current and future examination cycles should omit this chapter from their preparation schedules.

## Most-asked topics

Every historical problem in this chapter belongs to a single conceptual heading:

1. **Statements & Logic (101 questions, 2019-2026; 132 total, last asked 2023)**
Encompassed truth tables, tautologies, contradictions (fallacies), logical connectives including conjunction, disjunction, implication, and biconditional, contrapositives, and negations of compound propositions using De Morgan's laws.

## What the questions look like

Archived questions from 2019 to 2023 followed repetitive, standardized structures:

- **Tautology Verification**: Candidates were given four compound propositional statements involving implications and negations, and asked to identify which one evaluates to true under every possible truth assignment.
- **Negation and Contrapositive**: Finding the formal negation or the contrapositive of a conditional proposition expressed either in symbolic logic or in conversational English.
- **Equivalent Logical Form**: Simplifying a multi-bracketed logical expression using truth table values or boolean algebraic identities to identify an equivalent simpler proposition.
- **Operator Determination**: Choosing between logical AND or OR connectives for placeholder symbols so that a compound formula becomes a tautology.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Mathematical Reasoning tested in modern JEE Main papers?
No. The chapter was officially excised by the NTA starting with the 2024 examination session. It contributed zero questions across 2024, 2025, and 2026.

### Why was this chapter removed?
The NTA streamlined the mathematics syllabus to align more closely with revised rationalized secondary school curricula, eliminating isolated discrete units like logic while focusing on calculus, vectors, and coordinate geometry.

### How were these questions solved when they were tested?
Most students solved them by constructing a four-row truth table for two variables p and q, evaluating each sub-clause step by step to verify whether the final column consisted entirely of true values. Step-by-step solutions for each one are in the EdLoop app.
