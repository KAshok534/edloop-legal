## How often it comes up

NTA removed Mathematical Reasoning from the JEE Main syllabus in 2024. Across all papers we hold from 2008 to 2023, it contributed **132 questions across 200 papers**, appearing in 130 of those papers. From 2019 to 2026, it gave 101 questions in 162 papers, about 0.6 questions per paper.

Before its removal, the chapter was asked regularly. In the AIEEE and early JEE Main years (2002 to 2018) it gave 31 questions in 38 papers, making up 2.4% of Mathematics questions. In our set it was last asked in 2023.

It was historically taught as a Class 11 chapter under Mathematical Reasoning (removed 2024).

## What the questions look like

Questions in Mathematical Reasoning test both fundamental concepts and direct calculations. Students encounter problems that combine core definitions with multi-step algebraic or physical setups.

In **Statements & Logic**, questions focus on standard exam setups. Recent problems include: Simplify (~(P∧Q)) ∨ ((~P)∧Q) ⇒ ((~P)∧(~Q)); Statement equivalent to (p (p → q)). Success on these questions depends on understanding the underlying rules rather than relying on rote formulas.

A notable portion of this chapter appears in numerical-answer format: 2 of the 101 questions since 2019 had no options, requiring exact computation and proper handling of units.
