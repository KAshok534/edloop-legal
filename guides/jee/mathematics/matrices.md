## How often it comes up

Matrices is one of the most heavily tested chapters in JEE Main Mathematics. In the papers we hold from 2019 to 2026, it gave **260 questions in 162 papers**, about **1.6 questions per paper**, and it appeared in 158 of those papers. By share of Mathematics questions (5.7%) it ranks **4th of 29 Mathematics chapters** in that period.

The chapter has grown considerably since the AIEEE years. From 2002 to 2018 it was 4.0% of Mathematics, with 1.4 questions per paper; in the last three exam years (2024 to 2026) it is 5.7%, with 88 questions across the 58 papers we hold. The busiest year was 2021, with 46 questions across 26 papers. Since 2019 there has been no year without it, appearing in 158 of the 162 papers we hold.

It is a Class 12 chapter, part of NTA's Unit 3: Matrices and Determinants.

## Most-asked topics

Three topics cover the chapter almost evenly. From 2019 to 2026:

- **Systems of Linear Equations**: 104 questions. By far the most common use of matrices: when does a system have a unique solution, no solution or infinitely many solutions?
- **Inverse and Adjoint**: 93 questions. Properties of the adjoint and inverse, and identities involving determinants of powers and adjoints of adjoints.
- **Matrix Algebra**: 63 questions. Products, transpose, symmetric and skew-symmetric forms, and powers of matrices.

All three topics appeared in 2026.

## What the questions look like

Systems of linear equations questions give a 3 by 3 system with one or two parameters and ask for the value or range that makes the system consistent, inconsistent or dependent. The method is to form the coefficient matrix, row-reduce or evaluate the determinant, and read off the conditions.

Inverse and adjoint questions are the chapter's most distinct type. A common form gives a 3 by 3 matrix A with a known determinant and asks for the determinant of an expression like adj(2 adj A) or adj(adj A squared). These use the identity relating the determinant of the adjoint to the original determinant and do not require finding A explicitly.

Matrix algebra questions give specific 2 by 2 or 3 by 2 matrices and ask for products, eigenvalue-like conditions, or the number of matrices with given trace or entry constraints.

Of the 260 questions since 2019, 66 were numerical-answer type, so careful evaluation without rounding matters.

## How to practise it

Start with systems of linear equations. Set up each system as a matrix equation, reduce it and read the conditions on the parameters. This is the most testable skill and pays off in both Matrices and Determinants.

Then do inverse and adjoint. Memorise the two or three key identities: the determinant of the adjoint, the adjoint of a product, and the adjoint of the adjoint. Once those are fluent the numerical questions become short. Leave matrix algebra for last; it is the simplest topic and the questions are usually straightforward once you know how to multiply and transpose.

### Are Matrices and Determinants separate chapters?

Yes, they are listed as separate chapters on this site. The two chapters are related and their pages link to each other, but each has its own question list. Many students find it useful to study them together since the same matrix often appears in both types of questions.
